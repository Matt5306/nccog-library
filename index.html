How this works

  1. Add a sermon or book on the ➕ Add tab.
  2. The app reads it, picks the right notebook, and saves it on that notebook's next empty "shelf".
  3. Wait about 5 minutes. The notebook picks up new shelves by itself.
  4. Open the notebook from the 📚 Library tab and ask questions, or use Studio → Reports to make study guides and curricula.

Tips

  • YouTube videos must be public (or unlisted) for transcription to work.
  • If the app isn't sure which notebook fits, it will ask you to pick.
  • Anything made by the notebook is a first draft. Check quotes before teaching.

Something wrong?

Every error message says what happened and whether anything was saved. If you're stuck, email Matt at mstaton53@gmail.com with what you tapped and what you saw (a screenshot helps).
